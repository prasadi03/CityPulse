## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a welcoming, clear, and reassuring atmosphere for civic engagement and urban infrastructure monitoring. It moves away from cold bureaucratic styling and sterile data dashboards in favor of an inviting, optimistic civic environment reminiscent of a clear morning in an accessible city. 

The aesthetic blends warm minimalism with soft geometric forms:
- High atmospheric airiness with intentional breathing room.
- Soft cream canvas bases paired with pastel-tinted cards rather than harsh monochromatic surfaces.
- Strictly no dark mode, jet blacks, or severe charcoals; all deep values are anchored in comforting, warm dark umber tones to prevent visual intimidation.
- Human, approachable civic touchpoints that emphasize safety, transparency, and communal clarity.

## Colors

The color system operates on warm-bright optimism and gentle chromatic organization. Every token balances accessible contrast against an ambient, soft aesthetic.

### Functional Palette
- **Primary Canvas**: `#FFF9F2` (Warm off-white / soft cream). Serves as the global backdrop to eliminate glare and clinical sterility.
- **Card & Surface Backgrounds**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white) for crisp foreground definition, supported by soft tint containers:
  - Peach Tint: `#FFF0E6`
  - Butter Tint: `#FFF8D6`
  - Mint Tint: `#EAF8F0`
  - Sky Tint: `#E8F4FD`
- **Primary Accent**: `#FF9B71` (Warm Coral-Peach), transitioning to `#FF8456` on active interaction.
- **Secondary Accent**: `#5BB8F5` (Sky Blue) for secondary actions, information callouts, and civic infrastructure metrics.

### Typography & Content Color
- **Text Primary & Headings**: `#5A4A42` (Warm dark brown-gray). Provides strict contrast without the severe sharpness of pure black.
- **Text Secondary & Body**: `#7A6A62`.
- **Text Tertiary & Captions**: `#A89A92`.

### Municipal & Status Signals
- **Success (80–100 score)**: `#5FCB8A` (Fresh Green)
- **Warning (50–79 score)**: `#FFC93C` (Sunshine Yellow)
- **Critical (<50 score)**: `#FF6B6B` (Soft Coral Red)

### Domain-Specific System Badges
- **Electricity**: `#FFC93C` foreground on `#FFF8D6` container.
- **Water**: `#5BB8F5` foreground on `#E8F4FD` container.
- **Traffic**: `#FFA26B` foreground on `#FFF0E6` container.
- **Transport**: `#B7A6F5` foreground on `#F1EDFD` container.
- **Weather / Air Quality**: `#4FD1C5` foreground on `#E3F8F5` container.

### Boundaries
- **Structural Borders**: `#F0E6DF`.
- **Dividers & Hairlines**: `#F5EDE6`.

## Typography

The typography centers on Plus Jakarta Sans across all roles. Its geometric underpinnings combined with warm, humanist apertures maintain readability while feeling friendly and unbureaucratic.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display and Major Headlines**: Set in bold weights with tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to ground headers cleanly on airy canvases.
- **Section Titles and Component Labels**: Emphasize medium and semibold weights (`600`) to anchor cards, forms, and tabs without resorting to aggressive font-weight jumps.
- **Body Copy**: Standardized at regular (`400`) weight with generous line-heights (1.4x to 1.5x) to maintain effortless scannability across wide civic notices and metrics.
- **Tabular & Metric Data**: Numeric performance indices (such as city vitality scores) should use font features like tabular lining figures (`tnum`) when integrated into live-updating tiles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored in a responsive fluid grid that prioritizes breathing space and structural symmetry.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Mobile (< 640px)**: 4-column layout, `margin` of `1rem`, `gutter` of `1.25rem`. Complex metrics collapse into vertical stacked single-column units.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout, `margin-tablet` of `2rem`, `gutter` of `1.25rem`. Dashboards display in 2-column symmetric modules.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout with a maximum container limit of `1280px` centered within `margin-desktop` of `3rem`, `gutter-desktop` of `1.5rem`.

### Internal Rhythms
- Base layout rhythm scales on multiples of `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- Cards and content modules use `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) for standard interior padding to prevent elements from feeling crowded against soft, rounded borders.
- Sub-element groupings (e.g., metric label to metric numeric display) strictly apply `space-xs` (`0.25rem`) or `space-sm` (`0.5rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids dark drop shadows and heavy multi-layered stacking. Elevation is communicated primarily through subtle tonal contrast, soft boundary lines, and daylight-inspired ambient warmth.

### Elevation Styles
- **Flat Ground**: Canvas level `#FFF9F2`.
- **Resting Containers & Cards (Level 1)**: Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) or light system pastels bounded by a soft structural border (`1px solid #F0E6DF`). Elevated by an ambient, warm diffusion shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(122, 106, 98, 0.08);`
- **Interactive Surfaces & Hover States (Level 2)**: For clickable cards, floating action buttons, and active items:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(122, 106, 98, 0.12);`
  Slight upward translation of `-1px` on hover reinforces physical lightness.
- **Modals, Overlays & Popovers (Level 3)**:
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(122, 106, 98, 0.16);`
  Paired with a translucent warm wash backdrop: `rgba(90, 74, 66, 0.25)` accompanied by a `4px` backdrop blur.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a distinct shape language using pill geometry and generous card radiuses to eliminate aggressive corners and signal approachability.

### Radius Implementation
- **Cards, Panels & Modal Shells**: Defined with `rounded-2xl` (`1.5rem` to `2rem` / `24px` to `32px`), giving content blocks a gentle, friendly presence on the screen.
- **Action Elements & Badges**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`), including primary buttons, category tags, filters, and score pills.
- **Input Fields & Form Selectors**: High-radius surfaces (`1rem` / `16px`) that transition comfortably between structured rectangles and pure pills.
- **Icons & Embedded Media**: Nested surfaces within cards inherit an inner radius scaled proportionally down to `0.75rem` (`12px`) to maintain concentric alignment.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height of `44px` or `48px`, filled with `#FF9B71`, label in `#FFFFFF` with semibold weight. Hover state darkens to `#FF8456`. Focus ring is a clean `2px` offset with `#FF9B71`.
- **Secondary Button**: Pill-shaped, transparent or white background with `#F0E6DF` border, label in `#5A4A42`. Hover applies `#FFF0E6` background.
- **Ghost/Tertiary Button**: Pill-shaped, no border, text in `#7A6A62`, hover receives `#FFF0E6` tint.

### Chips & Badges
- Always pill-shaped with `height: 28px` to `32px` and `padding: 0 14px`.
- System category chips use thematic tint pairings:
  - Electricity: `#FFC93C` text on `#FFF8D6`
  - Water: `#5BB8F5` text on `#E8F4FD`
  - Traffic: `#FFA26B` text on `#FFF0E6`
  - Transport: `#B7A6F5` text on `#F1EDFD`
  - Air Quality: `#4FD1C5` text on `#E3F8F5`

### Cards
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` or contextual pastel tints.
- Border radius: `rounded-2xl` (`24px`).
- Border: `1px solid #F0E6DF`.
- Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(122, 106, 98, 0.08)`.
- Internal layout: Generous spacing (`space-lg` padding), pairing bold metric titles with secondary brown-gray metadata.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Height of `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #F0E6DF`, corner radius `1rem` (`16px`). Placeholder text set in tertiary `#A89A92`. Active focus sets border to `#FF9B71` with a `3px` diffuse glow `rgba(255, 155, 113, 0.2)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: `20px` width/height. Unchecked: `#FFFFFF` with `1.5px solid #F0E6DF`. Checked: `#FF9B71` fill with white check/dot icon. Radio radius is fully round; checkbox radius is `6px`.

### Lists
- Container list items use `padding: 1rem` separated by hairlines (`#F5EDE6`).
- Hover states on clickable rows reveal a smooth transition to `#FFF9F2`.

### Municipal Score Gauges (Product Specific)
- Numeric badges showcasing municipal quality scores:
  - High (80-100): `#5FCB8A` background with crisp white typography.
  - Caution (50-79): `#FFC93C` background with `#5A4A42` typography.
  - Alert (<50): `#FF6B6B` background with crisp white typography.
- Nested in `rounded-full` pill badges with `padding: 4px 12px` and bold numerical tracking.
